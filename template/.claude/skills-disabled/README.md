# Skills-disabled — the triage pool

Skills installed but deliberately NOT loaded (saves context, avoids misdirection).
To enable one: move its folder into `../skills/<name>/`. Record the decision in
`SKILL-SOURCES.md` (group, reason).

The pool ships with 13 dirs from the kit's seed triage — review them per your stack:
they are valid skills, just not worth their context cost in the seed project. Delete
what you will never use; enable what your stack needs.
